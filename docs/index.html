Legal

Legal intelligence engine

Answers questions across a whole contract library with every answer linked to its exact clause and page, and flags unusual or risky clauses so reviewers only look at what needs a second read. Retrieval is tuned on legal language specifically, so clauses that read alike but mean opposite things stop colliding.
